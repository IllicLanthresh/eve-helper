? Refined ISK/m³ prices each variant's exact reprocessing outputs (SDE data) at the section-2 prices and your per-ore refine yield from the facility row above. Compressed ISK/m³ prices the “Compressed …” type at Jita (fetched on demand, section-2 sell/buy basis) per m³ of raw rock; compression is 1:1 by units — one compressed unit per ore unit, only the volume shrinks (~100:1 ore, ~10:1 ice) — and the per-type unit ratio is derived from CCP's own reprocessing data rather than assumed. Raw ISK/m³ prices the untouched ore at its own Jita book. Toggling ISK/h swaps the per-m³ columns for hourly ones: same ranking, scaled by your yield. Compressed m³ is the haul volume after compression. Ice is unit-harvested and only ranks against other ice. With a sold by character chosen, every value column and everything derived from it is net of that character's sales tax — tax only, no broker fee, because the loot valuation assumes instant-style disposal.
