Structure Manager

One record per structure, shared by every tool. What is intrinsic to the structure lives here — the owner-set market broker rate, the facility tax, the installed rigs, the reprocessing rig, the hull's role bonuses, what it can build — and the Sell, Mine and Industry pages select a structure and read those facts. There is exactly one write-through: the Sell page's broker box, because that rate is read off the in-game market window while you are selling; typing it there records it here. What is yours rather than the structure's (which profile builds where, product scopes, your implant, cost-index overrides) stays in the tool that uses it.

What ESI does and doesn't tell us
  • Identity — name, hull type, system, security, region — is read from ESI and refreshed with the re-resolve button on each record. It is never hand-edited here.
  • Fittings are not in ESI, not even for the owner. Rigs are either picked from the real Standup catalog or worked out with infer rigs…, which reads the numbers the in-game industry window shows anyone who can use the structure.
  • Owner-set rates — market broker % and facility job tax % — have no ESI endpoint at all. Read them off the in-game market/industry window and type them in once, here.
  • Hull role bonuses are presets this app ships, not ESI data: correct one here and every Industry profile that routes work to this structure follows.
  • Every field is saved the moment you change it, and every open tab picks the change up.
