inventory → ESI prices → INSTANT / LIST / PATIENT · ranked by ISK/slot-day
?Paste an inventory, pick a hub, pull live prices from ESI (public, no login). Each item is valued against the real buy-order book (depth and min_volume scams respected) and against both ways of listing it, priced with the broker fee you pay up front and the odds the order ever fills. Ranking is expected ISK per market-slot-day: slots are the scarce resource. Long-form reference: README.
open orders per character · queue · fill odds · trend → HOLD / REPRICE / CANCEL & DUMP
?Every logged-in character's open orders from ESI, judged with the Sell mode's numbers: units queued ahead of you at your price, days for that queue to clear, the item's trend, and the odds of filling. Every order gets a verdict; the arithmetic is on the badge's tooltip. Long-form reference: README.

Notes & assumptions
  • INSTANT — walks the real buy book top-down; volume_remain and min_volume (margin-scam bait) respected. Certain, no broker fee, no market slot.
  • LIST at the competitive price L · PATIENT at the history reference. Value = p × (it fills) + (1 − p) × (dump at the end of the window, carried by the trend); the broker fee is subtracted in both branches, which is why a decaying item stops being recommended for listing.
  • SPLIT — a unit goes instant when its buy order beats L after fees: buys down to L × (1−tax−broker)/(1−tax) (≈ 0.977 L at default fees). Instant leg → checklist, remainder → import. Importing the whole stack at L is safe too: buys ≥ L fill at their own price, and only the sliver between the two thresholds is lost.
  • Chance % — share of past windows (7 / 14 / 30 d per patience, over ~12 months) whose daily high reached the price, each past day first carried to today's price level at the item's own trend, then tempered by the queue of cheaper sell orders. The carrying separates a dip from a decay. Under the floor (75 / 55 / 35%) a listing is never recommended — the broker fee would most likely be spent for nothing. Past behaviour, not a promise.
  • Fill est. — units already listed at or below the price ÷ Vol/day. Regional volume mixes both sides and every station, and your own stack is not counted in the queue: an optimistic order of magnitude, not a forecast.
  • ISK/slot-day — expected net ÷ expected days on the market. The ranking column, because market slots run out before ISK does. INSTANT uses no slot, has no value here, and sorts to the bottom of that column.
  • Vol/day — mean regional units traded per day over the history window, buys and sells combined (no API splits traded volume by side). Buy/Sell depth — the live book per side: outstanding orders right now, not traded volume. Margin % = (sell − buy) ÷ buy, negative only on a crossed market (already flagged). Sell/Hist % — live best sell vs the history statistic: green above, red below.
  • History is per region (ESI publishes none per station), on each day's average price. Day range and statistic are applied locally — changing them needs no refetch.
  • Sell u/d, Buy u/d and Sell share come from the Adam4EVE market order tracker, which samples the five hub order books every 10-15 minutes and differences them: units bought from sell orders are counted apart from units sold to buy orders, per station, per day. Only the first kind fills a sell order you leave up. It is an estimate — good on slow markets, weaker on fast ones — and it covers the five NPC hubs only. Refresh volume downloads it into your browser (no login); the line beside the button says how much history is held and how old it is. A first run over 90 days downloads 13 weekly files — about 62 MB gzipped, 2.1 million rows kept at the five hubs — and takes a couple of minutes; closing the tab keeps every day already stored. The window control quotes the download for each setting: 30d 21 MB, 90d 62 MB, 180d 124 MB, 365d 248 MB. Nothing is fetched until you ask for it, and with no cache the page falls back to the regional Vol/day above.
  • Flags, hover any for its numbers: ▼x%/wk falling trend · suspect top buy above best sell (thin/odd market) · ≫hist/≪hist best sell far from the history statistic · depth x/y the buy book absorbs x of your y units · minfee the 100 ISK per-order floor beats the broker % · dup×n you already have n sell orders for this item here — same queue, second broker fee, read from the My orders last pull · L=hist/L=sell the list price fell back to the other source · >best L above the best sell · no buy/no sell that side of the book is empty · unsellable Ice Storm / Expired filaments the market refuses, auto-excluded from the export (re-tick to include).
  • Fills are not guaranteed. Relist fees from repricing are not modelled on this side (the My orders mode does model them).
  • Every ESI endpoint used here is public: no login, and nothing leaves your machine but the price queries. Prices respect EVE's 4-significant-digit rule — a tick is one step of the price's own band.

Notes & assumptions
  • Source — /characters/{id}/orders/, per character, open orders only. A login without esi-markets.read_character_orders.v1 is listed above with a permissions link, never silently dropped from the totals.
  • Slots in use — a count of open orders per character. EVE's cap is a character fact (Trade / Retail / Wholesale / Tycoon plus skills) that ESI does not publish, so this counts what is open and invents no total.
  • Names — items from ESI, NPC stations from /universe/stations, player structures from your structure records or /universe/structures. A location your characters cannot resolve (no docking access, say) shows as its raw id, not a guess.
  • ISK tied up = price × units remaining. On a buy order that is ISK committed; real escrow can be lower once it is partly filled.
  • Queue ahead — units listed at or below your price there, your own remaining units taken back out (otherwise every order reads one stack worse than it is). vs best % compares against the best competing sell, so being cheapest reads 0%, not "you beat yourself".
  • Fill est. = queue ahead ÷ daily traded volume, exactly as in the Sell table and with the same caveat: regional volume mixes both sides and every station — an optimistic order of magnitude.
  • Chance % — the Sell mode's trend-adjusted hit rate: share of past windows whose daily high reached your price, each past day carried to today's level at the item's own trend, then tempered by the queue. Window = your patience or the days left on the order, whichever is shorter.
  • What is working against an order shows as a chip on its name, on any of: chance under the patience floor · the queue at your price cannot clear before expiry · you sit above the best sell on a falling market, where the gap only widens. Hover a chip for its numbers. It is evidence, not a second verdict.
  • Verdicts — three futures over the days left, all net of sales tax. HOLD = chance × (units × your price) + (1 − chance) × (dump at the end, carried by the trend) · REPRICE = the same at the competitive price, minus the relist fee · CANCEL & DUMP = what the buy book pays now, walked depth-first. The broker fee that put the order up is sunk: charged against none of the three, least of all against cancelling — the classic way to talk yourself into keeping a dead order.
  • Relist fee — not verified against the client. Modelled as the broker % from the fees box, less 5% per level of Advanced Broker Relations (resolved by name from your skills). Compare with your own modify-order dialog and type the real number into the box — it is saved.
  • Fetching — books and history per location and per type, grouped so a structure's book is pulled once however many orders sit in it. A missing book or history (no market scope at a structure, no docking access, a type with no history) leaves the row saying so and with no verdict, rather than a verdict built on nothing.
