Sell Helper

Paste your inventory, pick a trade hub, and pull live prices straight from ESI (no login). Every item is valued against the actual buy-order book — depth, min-volume scams and all — and against the two ways of listing it, priced with the fee you pay up front and the odds that the order ever fills. The recommendation is an action: sell it now, list it, or list it patiently — ranked by expected ISK per market-slot-day, because slots are what you run out of.

The orders you already have, pulled from ESI for every logged-in character, and judged against the same numbers the Sell mode uses: how many units are queued ahead of you at your price, how long that queue takes to clear, which way the price is going, and what the odds of filling actually are. Every stalled order gets a verdict — hold it, reprice it, or cancel and dump it — with the arithmetic behind it one hover away.

Notes & assumptions
  • Instant valuation walks the real buy book top-down: order depth (volume_remain) and min_volume (margin-scam bait) are respected, so one 1-unit buy order at a silly price no longer values your whole stack.
  • SPLIT is computed from the instant side: a unit goes to the instant leg whenever its buy order pays more after tax than listing it at L nets after tax + broker — i.e. buys down to L × (1−tax−broker)/(1−tax) (≈ 0.977 × L at default fees). The leg appears in the instant checklist (partial stack); the remainder is listed at L via the import. Importing the whole stack at L without selling the leg first is safe too — buys ≥ L fill automatically at their own price; you only lose the sliver between the two thresholds.
  • Order fills are not guaranteed — an order at a history-derived price may take days or never fill. Relist fees from repricing are not modelled.
  • Price history is per region (ESI has no station-level history) using each day's average price; the day range and statistic are applied locally, so changing them needs no refetch.
  • Vol/day is the mean of the region's daily traded units over the same "last N days" window as the history statistic (buys and sells combined — no API has per-side traded volume). The Buy/Sell depth columns are the live order book per side — outstanding orders right now, not traded volume. Margin % is (sell − buy) ÷ buy (negative only in crossed markets, which are already flagged). Sell/Hist % compares the live best sell to the history statistic — green above history, red below.
  • Three real disposals per item, each valued net of fees: INSTANT (dump into the buy book — certain, and it uses no market slot), LIST at the competitive price, and LIST-PATIENT at the history reference. A listing is worth p × (it fills) + (1 − p) × (you give up at the end of the window and dump it then, at a price carried by the trend) — and the broker fee is subtracted in both branches, because you pay it to put the order up. That is why a decaying item stops being recommended for listing.
  • Chance % is the empirical fill probability at the recommended price: over the last ~12 months, the share of rolling windows (7 / 14 / 30 days, per the patience control) in which the daily high reached that price — with every past day first carried to today's price level at the item's own trend, and then tempered by how long the queue of cheaper sell orders already is. The carrying is what separates a dip from a decay: on a sliding item the highs of last spring do not count as evidence about next week. Below the patience mode's floor (75% / 55% / 35%) a listing is never recommended — the broker fee would most likely be spent for nothing. Past behaviour is not a promise.
  • Fill est. = units already listed at or below the recommended price ÷ Vol/day. Regional traded volume mixes buy and sell sides and every station in the region, and your own stack is not counted in the queue, so treat it as an optimistic order-of-magnitude number.
  • ISK/slot-day = expected net ÷ expected days on the market. It is the ranking column because market slots, not ISK, are what you run out of. INSTANT rows have no value here — they use no slot — so they sort to the bottom of that column and live on the instant checklist instead.
  • suspect price = top buy above best sell (thin/odd market) · ≫/≪ history = current sell far from the history statistic · depth x/y = the buy book can only absorb x of your y units.
  • unsellable? = Ice Storm / Expired filaments the market won't accept; auto-excluded from the export (you can re-tick them).
  • All ESI endpoints used are public — no login, nothing leaves your machine except the price queries.
  • Prices respect EVE's 4-significant-digit rule (undercut = one tick of the price's own band).

Notes & assumptions
  • Orders are read per character from /characters/{id}/orders/, which lists only open orders. A character whose login does not carry esi-markets.read_character_orders.v1 is listed above with a permissions link — it is never silently left out of the totals.
  • Slots in use is a count of open orders per character. EVE's slot cap is a fact about the character (Trade / Retail / Wholesale / Tycoon plus skills you may not have trained), and ESI does not publish it, so this page counts what is open and does not invent a total.
  • Item names come from ESI; station names from /universe/stations and player structures from your structure records (or /universe/structures). A location your characters cannot resolve — a structure you have no docking access to, for instance — is shown as its raw id rather than a guess.
  • ISK tied up is price × units remaining. For a buy order that is the ISK committed to it; the actual escrow can be lower on a partly-filled order.
